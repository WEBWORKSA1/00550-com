About 00550

A friendly, independent guide to the numbers behind Chinese culture.

Our mission

More than a billion people read numbers as words. 8 means prosperity, 4 means death, 520 means “I love you”. We make that hidden language easy for everyone: couples choosing a wedding date, shoppers, travellers, students of Chinese, and businesses entering Chinese-speaking markets.

Why “00550”?

In number slang, 0 stands for 你 (you) and 5 for 我 (me). 00550 reads 你你我我你, “you, you, me, me, you”. It is a number about connection, which is what this site is for: connecting people with the culture behind the digits, and connecting brands with the audiences who care about them.

What we believe

  • Free first. Core tools stay free and require no sign-up.
  • Accurate and respectful. We note regional differences (Mandarin vs Cantonese) and avoid superstition-shaming.
  • Honest. Number symbolism is culture, not prediction. We never promise luck.
  • Transparent. Ads and sponsorships are always labelled.
