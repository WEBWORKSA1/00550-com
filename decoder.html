How to read your result

Score 85+: a very auspicious number, rich in 8, 6 and 9 with no 4s. 70–84: auspicious. 55–69: balanced, fine for everyday use. Below 55: contains numbers or pairs that many people avoid, such as 4, 14 or 74.

Why the last digit matters

Buyers of phone numbers and plates look hardest at the ending, because it's the part people remember. That's why numbers ending in 8 or 88 get a premium and our scoring weights the final digit twice.

Mandarin vs Cantonese

The same digit can sound lucky in one dialect and unlucky in another. We show both readings so you can judge for your audience. For high-stakes choices like brand launches, hotline numbers or pricing, ask for a human review.
