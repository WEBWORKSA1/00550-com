How number slang works

Each digit is swapped for a word that sounds similar: 5 (wǔ) → 我 wǒ “me”, 2 (èr) → 爱 ài “love”, 0 (líng) → 你 nǐ “you”. So 520 reads “I love you”. Codes spread through pagers in the 1990s, then QQ, Weibo and WeChat.
