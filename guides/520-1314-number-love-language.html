520 & 1314: China's numeric love language

Updated {{ site.time | date: "%Y-%m-%d" }} · 5 min read

520 = I love you

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ). That made May 20 an unofficial online Valentine's Day, with gifts, red envelopes of ¥520 and a rush of wedding registrations. 521 (我愿意, “I do”) is the classic reply, and May 21 gets celebrated too.

1314 = forever

1314 (yī sān yī sì) echoes 一生一世 (yīshēng yīshì, “one life, one world”). Here 4 reads 世 (world, lifetime), a rare positive 4. Put them together and 5201314 means “I love you forever”, and a transfer of ¥520.1314 is a popular romantic gesture.
