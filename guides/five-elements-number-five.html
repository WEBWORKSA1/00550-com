五行: the Five Elements

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water are less “elements” than phases of change. They describe how things grow, peak, settle, contract and rest. Each links to a season, direction, colour, organ and set of numbers.

Generating cycle: Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth (ash), Earth bears Metal, Metal carries Water (condensation), Water nourishes Wood. Controlling cycle: Wood parts Earth, Earth dams Water, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood.

五福: the Five Blessings

Longevity, wealth, health and peace, love of virtue, and a peaceful end of life. The five bats (蝠 fú, which sounds like 福 fú, blessing) you see on traditional art stand for these.

Five in everyday culture

  • Five tastes, five colours, five tones of the classical scale
  • Tiananmen's five arches and the imperial association of 5 and 9
  • Earth, the centre element, pairs with 5 and 0 in the traditional Hé Tú scheme, just like the digits of 00550.
