How Chinese families pick dates

  • Digits: dates with 6, 8 and 9 are favoured; 4 is avoided. Wedding registrations spike on 5/20 and dates containing 1314.
  • Ghost Month: the 7th lunar month is traditionally avoided for weddings, moves and openings.
  • Festivals: Qixi (7th day of the 7th lunar month) is the traditional lovers' day; Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn are family days.
  • Almanac (黄历): a traditional almanac lists “suitable” and “avoid” activities for each day by its stem-branch; formal date selection (择日) also considers the couple's birth charts.
