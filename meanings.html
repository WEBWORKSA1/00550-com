The special role of 0 and 5

0 (零 líng) sounds like 良 (liáng, good) and represents a circle, completeness, a fresh beginning. In slang it becomes 你 (you). 5 (五 wǔ) anchors the Five Elements and the Five Blessings (longevity, wealth, health, virtue and a peaceful end), and in slang it becomes 我 (me). Put them together and 00550 becomes a playful “you, you, me, me, you”.

Balance matters too. 55 reads as 呜呜 (crying) online, and Cantonese speakers hear 5 as 唔 (not). A good number reading always considers its audience. Read the 00550 deep dive.

Numbers in markets and money

Number symbolism has real prices. Phone numbers and license plates with repeated 8s are auctioned at large premiums in Chinese-speaking cities, many developers skip 4th, 14th and 24th floors, and retail prices often end in 8. Short numeric domain names are actively traded by Chinese-speaking investors because digits are easier to type and remember than romanised words.
