Privacy Policy

Summary

Our tools run in your browser: numbers, names and birth dates you type into the decoder, zodiac or date tools are not sent to our servers. We only receive information you choose to submit through forms.

Information we collect

  • Form submissions (name, email, message, and any other fields you fill in) for newsletters, audits, contests, donations, sponsorship inquiries and job applications. Forms are delivered through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox.
  • Usage data via cookies and similar technologies if you accept them (e.g. Google Analytics): pages viewed, device type, approximate location.
  • Local preferences such as dark mode and cookie choices, stored in your browser only.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose “Essential only”, we request non-personalised ads.

YouTube

Videos load only when you click play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (nocookie) mode.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • To send newsletters you opted into (unsubscribe anytime)
  • To run contests and contact winners
  • To process donation pledges and send payment links
  • To improve the site

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Payments are handled by third-party processors (such as PayPal or Stripe). We never see or store your card details.

Retention & your rights

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose collected. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, PIPL), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to processing, via our contact form.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contests require entrants to be of legal age.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
