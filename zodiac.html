The Five Elements (五行 Wǔ Xíng)

The number 5 is central to Chinese thought: five elements, five directions, five blessings (五福). Each lunar year pairs one of 12 animals with one of five elements (and yin or yang), giving a 60-year cycle.

Generating cycle: Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. Controlling cycle: Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood.

Number–element pairings follow the traditional Hé Tú (河图) scheme; feng shui schools vary.
